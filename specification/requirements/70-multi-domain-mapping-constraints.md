# Multi-Domain Mapping Constraint Requirements

**Family**: REQ-ODD-DOMAIN-MAPPING-CONSTRAINT-*
**Status**: Active
**Category**: Constraint / Guarantee

### REQ-ODD-DOMAIN-MAPPING-CONSTRAINT-001 — Downstream Of Published Truth

**Carries**:
- `WORLD_MODEL_METHOD.md` — `Constitutional Intents`, `Mappings Are Semantic Treatments`
- `INTENT.md` — `Constraints`
- `PRODUCT.md` — `Current Delivery Pillars`, `World-Model Query And Traversal`

The mapping line SHALL remain downstream of published domain artifacts and
composed world models. It SHALL not become a competing semantic source over the
domains it is mapping.

### REQ-ODD-DOMAIN-MAPPING-CONSTRAINT-002 — Single Durable Mapping Truth

**Carries**:
- `WORLD_MODEL_METHOD.md` — `Method Objectives`
- `INTENT.md` — `Constraints`
- `PRODUCT.md` — `Projection Layer`, `Conventional Projection`

The durable truth surface for a retained mapping slice SHALL be the published
mapping record. Any review analysis, confidence breakdown, or human-readable
report SHALL remain subordinate to that record or be explicitly marked as a
supporting constructive surface.

### REQ-ODD-DOMAIN-MAPPING-CONSTRAINT-003 — Identity And Loss Preservation

**Carries**:
- `WORLD_MODEL_METHOD.md` — `Mappings Are Semantic Treatments`
- `INTENT.md` — `Constraints`
- `PRODUCT.md` — `Core Product Intent / World-Model Composition`, `Product End State`

Every retained mapping record SHALL preserve source and target domain identity,
artifact version, local object or attribute refs, and declared loss or
ambiguity rather than flattening correspondence into anonymous target names.

### REQ-ODD-DOMAIN-MAPPING-CONSTRAINT-004 — Confidence Must Be Explainable

**Carries**:
- `WORLD_MODEL_METHOD.md` — `Method Objectives`
- `INTENT.md` — `Constraints`
- `PRODUCT.md` — `Product Vision`, `World-Model Query And Traversal`

Confidence for a mapping SHALL not be emitted as an opaque score only. The
authoritative confidence surface SHALL include an explainable band, stated
reasons, evidence basis, and unresolved ambiguity.

### REQ-ODD-DOMAIN-MAPPING-CONSTRAINT-005 — Treatment Before Name Similarity

**Carries**:
- `WORLD_MODEL_METHOD.md` — `Mappings Are Semantic Treatments`
- `INTENT.md` — `Constraints`
- `PRODUCT.md` — `Product Vision`, `Projection Layer`

The mapping line SHALL prioritize semantic treatment, object boundary, topology,
unit or basis compatibility, and supporting evidence over raw field-name or
label similarity when deciding cross-domain correspondence.

### REQ-ODD-DOMAIN-MAPPING-CONSTRAINT-006 — Generic Mapping Carrier

**Carries**:
- `ODD_METHOD.md` — `Core Law / 2. Graph Functions Are The Primary Constructive Carrier`
- `INTENT.md` — `Constraints`
- `PRODUCT.md` — `Current Product Definition`

The first mapping carrier may prove itself on one retained domain pair, but its
typed asset and graph-function boundaries SHALL be generic to published
world-model domains rather than hardcoded to one local trade or regulation
example.
