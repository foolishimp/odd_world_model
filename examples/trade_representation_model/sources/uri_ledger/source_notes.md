# Trade Representation Notes

This example input is the retained trade-representation source slice used by
the current example-domain proving corpus.

Its local shape is grounded by the official FpML authority carried by the
separate `trade_source_model` example domain.

The local semantic center is:

- trade contract state
- commodity or product representation
- counterparty identity
- governing agreement

The retained slice is sparse by design. It gives the builder enough material to
publish inspectable semantic objects inside the sandbox without pretending that
every treatment surface is already fully known.
