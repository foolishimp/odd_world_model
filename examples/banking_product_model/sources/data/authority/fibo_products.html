FIBO Products

About

FIBO is intended for a range of uses, including in its simplest form as an English language glossary or a bank or regulator's data dictionary. In its most complex form, FIBO OWL, could be used as a bank's operational ontology.

FIBO is also available as derived products. All FIBO products are generated or derived with an automated process from the FIBO OWL. Thus all changes in the FIBO OWL will be reflected in all other products. There are also external uses of FIBO, such as FIBO.schema.org, which use FIBO as a reference for search indexing of specific information on the Internet.
